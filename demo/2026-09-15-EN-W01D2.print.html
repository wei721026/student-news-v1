Can AI Help Scientists Read the Moon?

NASA and IBM released a new AI tool for Moon science. It helps researchers work with huge amounts of lunar data.
NASA and IBM released an open-source AI model for lunar science. It can help researchers map craters, study volcanic features, and estimate places where ice may be stable near the Moon's poles.

Focus sentence
Because the model is already trained on lunar data, scientists can adapt it to map craters and estimate where ice may be stable.

What did NASA and IBM release?

The NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model is an open-source AI model for Moon research. NASA says it was trained mainly on Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data, including roughly two million image tiles from years of observations.

What is the model mainly designed to help scientists do?
  1. Analyze large amounts of lunar data
  2. Move the Moon closer to Earth
  3. Replace every lunar scientist
Answer: Analyze large amounts of lunar data

What can the model help study?

Researchers can adapt the model to map craters, spot unusual volcanic features, and estimate where ice may be stable near the Moon's poles. That word estimate matters: the model can point to promising places, but a prediction is not the same as direct proof of ice.
